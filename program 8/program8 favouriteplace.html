My Favourite Place


MUNNAR

Munnar is a beautiful hill station in the Idukki district of Kerala. It is famous for its green tea plantations, misty mountains, pleasant climate, and beautiful valleys. The hills covered with tea gardens make Munnar a wonderful place to visit. The town is surrounded by forests, waterfalls, and many scenic places.

Munnar is also known for its rich wildlife and natural beauty. The rare Nilgiri tahr can be found in the nearby Eravikulam National Park. Tourists enjoy activities such as trekking, sightseeing, boating, and visiting tea museums. Munnar is a peaceful and attractive destination and is often called one of the most beautiful places in Kerala.
