Paradise Resort

Paradise Resort Kumarakom is a serene backwater retreat located in the beautiful Kumarakom region of Kerala, overlooking the tranquil Vembanad Lake. Surrounded by lush greenery and tropical landscapes, the resort offers comfortable accommodation, a swimming pool, restaurant, boating facilities, and other modern amenities. Its peaceful setting, scenic backwater views, traditional Kerala ambience, and proximity to Kumarakom’s famous bird sanctuary and waterways make it an ideal destination for a relaxing and memorable getaway.
